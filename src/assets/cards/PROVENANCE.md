# Card face images

The 52 SVG card faces in this folder are Byron Knoll's "Vector Playing
Cards", released into the public domain (with a WTFPL fallback for
jurisdictions without a public-domain concept).

Source: https://github.com/notpeter/vector-playing-cards (the maintained
mirror of the original Google Code project,
https://code.google.com/p/vector-playing-cards/).

Changes from the source: files are renamed from the mirror's short names
(e.g. AC.svg) to this project's rank-suit keys (e.g. A-clubs.svg); the
two joker images were not copied. The image contents are unmodified.
